# Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an Excel-based Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across products, categories, months, locations, and time of day.
The dashboard provides a simple visual overview of key sales metrics and helps identify high-performing categories, locations, and sales periods.

## Dashboard Preview
![Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard](dashboard/coffee_shop_sales_dashboard.png)

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: $23,653
- Coffee Sales: $9,589
- Tea Sales: $6,937
- Bakery Sales: $2,617
- Coffee Share: 41%
- Tea Share: 29%
- Bakery Share: 11%

## Analysis Included
The dashboard contains:
- Total sales amount
- Sales by product category
- Quantity sold by category
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by day/time
- Store location performance
- Top 5 categories
- Order-of-the-month information

## Dashboard Insights
Based on the dashboard:
1. Coffee is the strongest category by sales, contributing approximately 41% of the displayed category sales.
2. Tea is the second-largest category with approximately 29%.
3. The monthly graph shows an overall upward sales trend from January through June.
4. Morning sales represent the largest portion of sales in the day-time chart.
5. The dashboard compares performance across Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan.
6. Coffee and Tea are the leading categories by sales value.
